### 1. PURPOSE OF THE STUDY
You are cordially invited to participate in a capstone research study conducted by undergraduate researchers from the **College of Computer Studies** at the **University of Perpetual Help System DALTA – Molino Campus**.

This study aims to develop, calibrate, and evaluate **CogniCore**, a web-based adaptive serious game designed for cognitive skill assessment, training, and behavioral analytics. Specifically, the system utilizes an **Item Response Theory (IRT)-based Dynamic Difficulty Adjustment (DDA)** engine to tailor game difficulty in real time to match player skill, and a **Machine Learning Random Forest Classifier** to assess individual cognitive archetypes across four core cognitive domains:
1. **Spatial-Visual Memory** (pattern retention, object-location association)
2. **Reflex & Attentional Focus** (reaction speed, selective attention, cognitive inhibition)
3. **Logical-Mathematical Reasoning** (sequence deduction, inductive logic, computational balance)
4. **Executive Strategy & Planning** (cognitive flexibility, rule-switching, priority allocation)

The findings of this study will contribute to academic research in Artificial Intelligence in Education (AIEd), Human-Computer Interaction (HCI), psychometric behavioral telemetry, and adaptive serious gaming.

### 3. PROCEDURES INVOLVED
If you agree to take part in this study, you will complete the following activities under the guidance of the researchers:

1. **System Onboarding & Demographics (approx. 5 minutes):**
   * Register with a unique, non-identifying participant username and a private 4-digit security PIN.
   * Provide basic non-identifying demographic data (age, course/academic program, gaming familiarity).
2. **Cognitive Baseline Pre-Test (approx. 10 minutes):**
   * Complete a standardized multiple-choice baseline assessment covering all four cognitive domains to establish your initial cognitive benchmark score.
3. **Adaptive Gameplay Training Sessions (approx. 20–25 minutes):**
   * Play a series of interactive mini-games on a desktop/laptop computer (e.g., *Speed Tap*, *Focus Finder*, *Matrix Recall*, *Logic Link*, *Rule Shifter*, etc.).
   * Each gameplay round is strictly standardized to **60 seconds**.
   * During gameplay, the system passively logs behavioral telemetry (reaction times, accuracy rates, error counts, hesitation latency, and path efficiency) and dynamically adjusts game difficulty to maintain your individual flow state.
4. **Cognitive Post-Test & Dashboard Review (approx. 10 minutes):**
   * Retake the cognitive assessment to determine personal performance differences across the 4 domains.
   * View your personalized AI-driven cognitive profile report, radar charts, and longitudinal archetype classification (*Fast Learner*, *Steady Improver*, or *High Fatigue*).
5. **Focus Group Discussion (FGD) & Survey Evaluation (approx. 15–20 minutes):**
   * Complete the **ISO/IEC 25010 Software Quality Questionnaire** (assessing functionality, usability, reliability, efficiency) and the **Game Experience Questionnaire (GEQ)** (assessing flow, immersion, challenge, and affect).
   * Participate in an informal group discussion sharing your thoughts on game feel, difficulty balancing, visual clarity, and responsiveness.

* **Estimated Total Duration:** Approximately **60 to 75 minutes** (can be conducted in a single session or across two scheduled sessions).
* **Mode & Location:** In-person at the **College of Computer Studies Computer Laboratory**, University of Perpetual Help System DALTA – Molino Campus.
* **Recording:** The Focus Group Discussion segment may be audio-recorded solely for transcribing qualitative feedback and system recommendations. You may choose to opt out of audio recording without affecting your participation.

### 5. POTENTIAL RISKS AND DISCOMFORTS
This study involves only minimal risks, not exceeding those encountered during everyday computer or video game usage:
* **Eye Strain & Cognitive Fatigue:** Concentrating on fast-paced interactive challenges may cause temporary ocular or mental tiredness.
* **Mild Performance Frustration:** Timed rounds and adaptive difficulty scaling may occasionally cause temporary challenge-related tension.
* **Visual Stimulation:** Certain mini-games include moving geometric patterns and rapid color cues.

#### Risk Mitigation Measures:
* **Built-in Time Limits:** All individual game trials are capped at **60 seconds**, preventing prolonged continuous cognitive strain.
* **Accessibility Settings:** An optional **"Reduce Flashes"** mode is available in the profile settings to disable high-contrast strobe or flashing visual effects.
* **Instant Pause & Rest Intervals:** Mandatory 2-to-3 minute rest breaks will be provided between training modules.
* **Non-Clinical Guarantee:** CogniCore is explicitly designed as a formative educational/training serious game and is **not** a clinical diagnostic tool. Scores will never be used for psychiatric or medical evaluation.
* **Immediate Supervision:** Researchers will be physically present at all times to monitor participant comfort and assist with technical issues.

### 7. CONFIDENTIALITY AND DATA PROTECTION
In strict compliance with the **Philippine Republic Act No. 10173 (Data Privacy Act of 2012)**:
* **Anonymity & Pseudonyms:** Real names and student numbers will **never** be stored in the game database. You will be identified only by an anonymized participant code (e.g., `FG-P01` to `FG-P20`) or a chosen username.
* **Telemetry Data Logged:** Only interaction telemetry relevant to the study will be recorded (timestamps, response times in milliseconds, accuracy percentages, game level, hesitation times, and survey ratings).
* **Data Security:** All backend databases are hosted on an encrypted cloud infrastructure (PostgreSQL on Supabase) utilizing **Row Level Security (RLS)** and SSL encryption. Authentication PINs are salted and hashed using Bcrypt.
* **Access Limitations:** Only the primary researchers and the research adviser will have access to raw data spreadsheets.
* **Retention and Disposal:** All collected digital telemetry, evaluation records, and audio recordings will be stored securely for the duration of the capstone thesis defense and validation period (Academic Year 2025–2026), after which all digital logs will be permanently deleted and purged.
